# Parallax Launcher - Style 7: Motorola RAZR V3i (Synergy OS)

> **Design Persona:** Motorola RAZR V3i (2005/2006) / Moto Synergy OS / Laser-Etched Clamshell.
> **Vibe:** Aircraft-grade spun aluminum, electro-luminescent cobalt-blue keypad glow, classic 2G/GPRS status bars, vintage carrier branding (Jio -> Reliance Communications, Vi -> Hutch), 9-icon Synergy main menu, and tactile D-Pad navigation.

---

## 1. Vintage Telecom & Off-the-Grid Carrier Spoofing
- **Forced 2G/GPRS Telephony Aesthetic:**
  - Signal meter shows 1–4 bars with vintage `[G]` (GPRS) or `[E]` (EDGE) stamp.
  - Never displays 4G/5G/LTE.
- **2005 Era Carrier Name Translation:**
  - `Jio` / `Reliance Jio` ➔ **`Reliance Communications`** (or `Reliance Infocomm`)
  - `Vi` / `Vodafone` / `Idea` ➔ **`HUTCH`**
  - `Airtel` ➔ **`AirTel 2G`**
  - `BSNL` ➔ **`CellOne`**
  - `AT&T` ➔ **`Cingular`**
  - `Verizon` ➔ **`Verizon Wireless`**
  - `T-Mobile` ➔ **`VoiceStream`**
  - `Sprint` ➔ **`Sprint PCS`**

---

## 2. Iconic Visuals & UI Structure
1. **RAZR Internal Color Display (176x220 / 240x320 Ratio):**
   - Metal bezel frame with silver Motorola "Batwing" logo at top.
   - Status bar: Signal bars, GPRS badge, Roaming, Message indicator, 3-block Battery icon.
   - Large Motorola Digital Clock (`HH:mm`) with blue shadow.
   - 4-way D-Pad softkey cross overlay:
     - Left: Messages
     - Right: Camera
     - Up: Phonebook
     - Down: Recent Calls
     - Center: Menu
   - Bottom Softkey Bar: `[ Message ]` • `[ Menu ]` • `[ Contacts ]`.

2. **Motorola 9-Icon Synergy Main Menu:**
   - 3x3 Grid with classic icons:
     1. Phonebook
     2. Recent Calls
     3. Messages
     4. Games & Apps (Opens full app drawer)
     5. Multimedia
     6. WebAccess
     7. Tools / Calculator
     8. Settings
     9. Camera
   - D-Pad navigable with sound effects and animated highlight box.

3. **Laser-Cut Electro-Luminescent Keypad:**
   - Chemically etched nickel-plated sheet metal with cyan-blue luminescent backlit grooves (`#00E5FF`).
   - Integrated D-Pad, softkeys, Call/End keys, and 12-key numeric dial pad.
